### Editor fonts

The plugin's editor and the web app embed two fonts from `assets/fonts/`, unmodified, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`src/gui/fonts.rs`):

- **Inter 4.1, Regular** (`Inter-Regular.ttf`) by The Inter Project Authors (<https://github.com/rsms/inter>). It sets the whole interface.
